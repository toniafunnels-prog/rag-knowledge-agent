# Sample Company Policy Document (Demo Data)

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.
All remote employees must be reachable during core hours: 10am-3pm in their local timezone.

## Expense Reimbursement
Expenses under $50 do not require pre-approval. Expenses between $50-$500 require
manager approval before purchase. Expenses over $500 require both manager and
finance team approval. Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days
of the expense date, via the internal expense portal.

## PTO Policy
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per year, increasing to 20 days after
3 years of tenure. PTO requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
for planned leave. Sick leave is separate and does not count against PTO balance,
capped at 10 days per year.

## Onboarding Process
New hires complete a 2-week onboarding program covering: company systems access
(day 1), team introductions (week 1), role-specific training (week 1-2), and a
30-day check-in with their manager. IT equipment is provisioned before the
employee's start date whenever possible.
